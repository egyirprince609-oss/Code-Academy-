Lesson Orientation
STEP 1 / 6

Learn in focused pages

Lesson content is arranged intelligently. Sentences stay together when there is enough space. When the screen becomes full, the next content automatically moves to a fresh page.

STEP 2 / 6

Your hearts are your lives

A wrong quiz answer removes one heart. Correct answers do not remove hearts. Once all five hearts are gone, you cannot continue until you refill or wait for recovery.

STEP 3 / 6

XP determines qualification

Every quiz question has its own XP reward. Your lesson score is based on the XP you actually earn from the questions.

STEP 4 / 6

Earn at least half

You need at least 50% of the total possible quiz XP for this lesson to qualify for the next lesson. Less than half means you repeat the lesson.

STEP 5 / 6

Practice the code

Code examples are copyable and designed to help you practice what you are learning. You can also use the side games and coding practice from the menu when you need more XP.

STEP 6 / 6

Learn attentively

Questions are recorded in your history and question rewards are protected from duplicate XP. Answer carefully and complete the lesson with at least half of its available XP.
