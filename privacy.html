This document is written for the public Code Academy website. Read it alongside the other policy and the explanations shown in the product. The actual features and data practices of the live service must match what is described here.

1. Overview

Code Academy is a learning platform for programming lessons, practice activities, coding projects, progress tracking, and related learning features. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website, create an account, use Google sign-in, save work, participate in referrals, or contact support. Please read it before using account-based features.

2. Who operates Code Academy

Code Academy is an independently developed educational project. For privacy questions or requests, contact egyirprince609@gmail.com. This page is intended to explain the current website's practices in plain language; it is not a promise that every feature described as optional is enabled on every page.

3. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose “Sign in with Google,” Google authenticates you and shares the basic profile information authorized by the sign-in configuration. Depending on the account and permissions provided, this can include your display name, email address, profile photo URL, and a unique account identifier supplied to the authentication service. Code Academy uses the information needed to create or recognize your account, associate your learning profile with it, and display account details within the service.

How Google data is used

We use Google sign-in information only to authenticate you, create or maintain your Code Academy account, identify your account within the platform, and display your name or profile image where the interface offers that feature. We do not use Google sign-in to read your Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, calendar contents, or other Google services unless a future feature separately requests access, explains why, and obtains the required permission. The current sign-in flow is not intended to collect your Google password; authentication is handled by Google and Firebase Authentication.

Google API Services Limited Use disclosure

Code Academy’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or transfer it to third parties for their independent advertising purposes. We do not allow people to read Google user data except when necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, comply with law, or address security issues, and only as permitted by applicable policy and your consent.

4. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

Account and profile details

Your name or display name, email address, Firebase Authentication user identifier, profile-photo URL if supplied by Google or uploaded by you, account creation time, and account settings. For email/password registration, Firebase Authentication processes the email address and authentication credentials. Code Academy does not receive or store your Google password.

Learning activity and saved work

Learning progress, completed lessons, quiz results or scores, experience points (XP), coins, streaks, certificate status or certificate records, course preferences, referral code and referral-related records, and projects or code you choose to save. Some learning preferences or progress may be stored in your browser's local storage; account-linked records and saved projects may be stored in Firebase Firestore. Do not include passwords, private keys, payment details, or other secrets in projects or messages.

Messages you send us

If you contact support by email, WhatsApp, or another channel, we may receive the contact details and message content you choose to send. The third-party channel you use has its own privacy practices.

Technical and usage information

The hosting, authentication, database, and browser technologies used by the website may process technical information needed to deliver the service, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain security. Depending on the provider, this may include IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, request logs, and security events. Code Academy does not promise to receive every such item directly; the relevant service provider may process it as part of providing its service.

5. Location, time zone, and calendar dates

The Live Map page can request device geolocation only after you tap “Locate me” or “Start live GPS” and grant the browser or operating-system permission. It may display latitude, longitude, reported horizontal accuracy, GPS altitude and motion data where the device supplies them. Location precision depends on the device, signal, environment and sensor quality; Code Academy does not guarantee survey-grade accuracy. Live updates run while the page is active and are stopped when you turn them off or leave the page; this web page does not silently track in the background. A device-compass reading is optional and may be approximate or unsupported.

To render the map, search for a destination, request place labels or perform reverse-address lookup, the page sends the relevant map, search or coordinate request to its map provider. The 2D fallback uses OpenStreetMap map tiles and its reverse-geocoding service; the enhanced 3D/terrain mode uses Mapbox when the site is configured with a public Mapbox token. Providers may process the request according to their own terms and privacy policies. Map imagery, building footprints, labels, satellite photos and elevation are supplied by providers, may be dated or incomplete, and are not a live camera feed. The map cannot show a person's front/back view or reveal every house, car or material in real time.

Exact location sharing through a map link requires a separate action and confirmation. Device notifications can show a location summary only after you grant notification permission; depending on the phone settings, a notification preview may be visible on the lock screen. When you are signed in, the page adds activity records to your Code Academy notification feed. Exact coordinates are not included in those account notifications unless you turn on the separate “Include coordinates” option on the Live Map page. A confirmed location share may reveal precise coordinates to recipients, so share only with people you trust. Map preferences and any locally entered Mapbox public token are saved in this browser, not in your account profile.

Your device or browser may independently know your time zone or locale. Pages that display dates or certificate times may use the device's current date/time or browser locale to format dates.

6. Cookies, local storage, and similar technologies

The website may use browser local storage to remember selected preferences, course progress, referral information, temporary interface state, or local recovery copies. Firebase Authentication and related services may use browser storage or similar mechanisms to keep you signed in and protect the session. You can clear website data in your browser, but doing so may sign you out or remove locally saved progress and recovery data. Blocking storage may cause some features to stop working.

7. How information is shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that make Code Academy work, including GitHub Pages for hosting and Google/Firebase services for authentication, database storage, and any configured file storage. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy documentation. We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect users or the service, investigate abuse, or enforce our terms. We do not disclose personal information to classmates or other users unless a feature expressly displays it or you choose to share it.

8. Where information is stored and how long we keep it

Account-linked data may be stored in Firebase services configured for Code Academy. Some information is stored only in your browser. We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain learning records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or protect the platform. We do not promise a fixed deletion period for every record. Deleting browser storage removes local copies but may not delete server-side account data.

9. Your choices and requests

You can choose whether to use Google sign-in, edit available profile details, avoid optional features, and clear local browser storage. You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account. We may need to verify that you control the account before acting. Some records may need to be retained for security, legal, or legitimate operational reasons. If you use Google sign-in, you can also review and manage third-party access from your Google Account settings.

10. Security

We use the security features provided by our hosting and Firebase services and aim to limit access to account data through appropriate application configuration. No website, network transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your account credentials private, use a secure device, sign out on shared devices, and report suspected account misuse.

11. Third-party websites and services

Code Academy may link to external websites, tools, or learning resources. We do not control their content or privacy practices. When you follow an external link, review the policies of that provider. Google, Firebase, GitHub, and communication providers may publish separate privacy terms that also apply to their processing.

12. Students and younger users

Code Academy is an educational project that may be used by students. If you are under the age required to consent to online services in your location, use the service with the involvement of a parent, guardian, or school where required. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information about yourself or other people. Parents or guardians who have privacy questions may contact us using the address below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features, providers, or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made. Continued use after an update means the updated policy applies to future use, to the extent permitted by law. Where required, we will provide an additional notice or request consent.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about Code Academy’s handling of information, email egyirprince609@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, authentication codes, or copies of identity documents unless we have explained a legitimate need and a safe method for providing them.
