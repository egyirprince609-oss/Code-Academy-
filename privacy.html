Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Code Academy may handle information when you browse the public site, create an account, learn, play coding games, create projects or contact support.

1. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Code Academy website and its learning features operated at egyirprince609-oss.github.io/Code-Academy-. It covers the information that may be processed through the website, account sign-in, learning activities, project features and support channels. External websites and services have their own privacy terms.

2. Information we may collect or process

The information depends on which features you choose to use. It may include:

  • Account and profile data: email address, account identifier, display name, profile image or other details supplied through an enabled sign-in provider.
  • Authentication data: information required to create, authenticate, maintain and secure your account. Password handling is managed by the configured authentication provider; do not email or message your password to us.
  • Learning and activity data: course or lesson completion, quiz answers or scores, game results, points, certificates, preferences and progress where those features save or transmit such information.
  • User-created content: code, project names, files or other content you choose to enter, save or submit through a feature.
  • Support communications: the email address and message content you provide when requesting assistance.
  • Technical information: hosting or security logs and technical details that service providers may process, such as IP address, browser, device, timestamps, diagnostic events and request information.

We do not claim to collect every category listed above in every session. Whether information is collected depends on the feature, your actions and the services enabled.

3. Purposes and lawful reasons for processing

Information may be used to provide requested account and learning functions, display or preserve progress, operate quizzes and games, support project features, issue or verify certificates, respond to enquiries, diagnose failures, protect accounts, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Where applicable law requires consent or another legal basis, processing should rely on the appropriate basis for the relevant activity.

4. Authentication, hosting and service providers

Code Academy is hosted through GitHub Pages and may use Firebase Authentication and other Firebase services configured for the app. When you use a sign-in method, the relevant provider processes information needed to authenticate you. Hosting, security and database providers may process or store data necessary to deliver the requested feature. Their own privacy notices and terms also apply. Review the project's live configuration to identify every active provider and service.

5. Cookies, local storage and similar technologies

The website or its libraries may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies to remember settings, support authentication, retain local state, improve reliability or protect the service. The exact technologies depend on the current implementation and third-party services. You can manage browser storage through your browser settings; clearing it may sign you out or remove locally stored preferences or progress.

6. Disclosure and sharing

We do not sell personal information for money. Information may be disclosed to service providers when needed to operate the website, when you choose to use an integrated service, when required by law or lawful process, or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, security or legal rights. Information you intentionally post in a public area may be visible to others; do not publish passwords, private contact details or confidential material in public content.

7. Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including providing the feature, preserving requested progress, maintaining security, resolving disputes or meeting legal obligations. Actual deletion timing may depend on the service and database configuration. To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with your account, contact us using the details below and identify the account without sending your password.

8. Security and responsibility

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information, including appropriate access controls and secure configuration of the services used. No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users must protect their sign-in credentials, use trusted devices where possible and promptly report suspected unauthorized account activity.

9. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, or to object to particular processing. You may choose not to create an account, and you can stop using the service. We will review privacy requests and respond as required by applicable law; identity verification may be necessary before acting on an account request.

10. Children and young users

Code Academy is an educational service. Users should provide only information they are allowed to share and should involve a parent or guardian where required by local law. We do not intend this policy to authorize collection of children's personal information contrary to applicable law. If you believe information was provided inappropriately, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

11. International processing and third-party links

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their systems and applicable safeguards. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience; we do not control their content, security or privacy practices. Read their policies before submitting information.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the website, services or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes should be highlighted where reasonably practical.

13. Contact and privacy requests

For questions or requests relating to this policy, email egyirprince609@gmail.com. Please describe your request clearly and do not include passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information.
