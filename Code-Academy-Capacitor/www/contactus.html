Quick answers

Will the message send automatically?

No. The page opens WhatsApp or your email app with a prepared draft. Review it and press Send there.

Why do I need to sign in?

Support is for registered Code Academy members. Signing in also lets us associate the report with your account if the save is permitted.

What if Firebase cannot save the request?

The page still attempts to open your chosen app with the report. The message is not considered sent until you send it there.

Can I include my password?

No. Never send a password, one-time code, recovery phrase, or private payment credentials. Support will not need them.

Privacy reminder

Your preferred contact details are used in the report so the support team can reply. The page does not print the support destination on screen. Because this is a static website, however, its message destination cannot be made secret from someone who inspects the page source; a trusted server endpoint would be needed to conceal it fully.
