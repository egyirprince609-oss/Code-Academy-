How referrals work

01
Copy your link. Your personal referral link is generated from your existing account ID.
02
Share with friends. Use WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Telegram, LinkedIn or the native share menu.
03
Your friend joins. The referral link includes your existing ?ref= parameter. The signup process must record the referral correctly.
04
Referral rewards. This page retains your existing display calculation of 100 XP and 10 coins per recorded referral.
05
Reward protection. Your signup and Firebase referral rules remain responsible for validating referrals and preventing duplicate rewards.
