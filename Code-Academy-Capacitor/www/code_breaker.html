Know the rules before you play

1. Find the problem

Read the prompt and code carefully. Choose the best answer from the options. Challenges become harder as you progress.

2. Use hints wisely

A hint costs 50 XP and 25 coins. Your balance is checked before a hint is revealed, and hint use is recorded in your history.

3. Earn rewards

Complete a question you have not completed before to earn 10 XP and 5 coins. Re-answering an old question does not pay rewards twice.
