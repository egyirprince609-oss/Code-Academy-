Browser works locally even when you are signed out. Bookmarks and settings can sync to your existing Code Academy Firebase UID. Browsing history is never uploaded unless you enable history sync inside browser settings.

Privacy at a glance
✓Websites load directly from your device through Android WebView. Code Academy does not proxy normal site traffic or run its own DNS service.
✓Website cookies and login sessions stay in the browser profile. They are not copied into Firebase browser records.
✓Invalid TLS certificates are blocked. HTTPS means the connection is encrypted to that site; it does not prove that the site is honest.
✓Private-session tabs are not saved to normal history or cloud sync. Android, networks and visited sites may still observe some activity; private mode is not anonymity.
