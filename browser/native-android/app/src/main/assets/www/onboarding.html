YOUR FIRST BROWSER SESSION

Welcome to your web workspace.

Let us walk through the features. This built-in guide uses prepared help content and works without an external AI key; it does not pretend to be a live AI model.

01 · SEARCHSearch or open a website

Type a question or a website address into the address bar. HTTP and HTTPS addresses are allowed; unsafe URL schemes are blocked.

02 · TABSKeep pages together

Use the plus button to create tabs, the tab strip to switch pages and the history drawer to revisit recent searches.

03 · PRIVACYControl what gets saved

Open the menu and Settings to change local history, private session, safe-search preferences, and supported device permissions.

04 · ACCOUNTYour browser profile

Your sign-in uses the Code Academy authentication module. Browser preferences and data use a browser-specific profile collection; cloud sync depends on your deployed Firebase rules.

05 · FULL WEBSITESOpen real website pages

Enter a domain such as google.com or paypal.com. The Android app opens the site in its native WebView. In a PWA, websites that refuse iframe embedding open in a full browser tab instead.

06 · ERRORSSee the reason when something fails

Search and news errors show the source error, retry controls and matching network/site illustrations. A webpage cannot reveal another site’s hidden HTTP status from a blocked cross-origin iframe.

Ask the built-in guide

Try: “How do I clear history?”, “How do I change search engine?”, “How do I keep my tabs private?” or “Why might a site not open inside the page?”
