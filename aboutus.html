Our purpose

More than reading lessons

The goal is to make the learning process active, understandable, and connected to building things.

Code Academy is a growing, independently developed learning app for people who want to understand programming by doing it. Lessons introduce concepts in manageable steps; quizzes help check understanding; games offer additional logic practice; and the code editor and projects area provide a place to experiment.

It is built for learners who may be using a phone as their main device as well as people working on larger screens. The interface is designed to adapt to different screen sizes and keep the important learning tools easy to reach.

The app continues to evolve. Course materials, games, editor features, and the surrounding tools may be improved over time. The aim is not to promise instant mastery, but to make it easier to practise regularly and recognise what you are learning.

“We Learn, We Code, We Succeed.”

The idea behind Code Academy is simple: learn a concept, test your understanding, build something small, then improve it.

ⓘBuilt to keep growing. Some integrations depend on an internet connection, a signed-in account, device support, or the availability of a suitable runtime.
